CLEAR RESPONSIBILITIES

Approve the action.
Let execution follow.

Your account decides who can authorize. A separate fee wallet pays to put the approved action on-chain.

Understand the signing flow →
  1. 01

    Choose what happens

    The dashboard prepares a specific intent: recipients, amounts and policy effects.

  2. 02

    Approve with the right keys

    The account policy selects the required approvals. Your phone or wallet signs the intent.

  3. 03

    Fund, submit, confirm

    A fee wallet signs the transaction. The ledger validates the rules before assets move.
